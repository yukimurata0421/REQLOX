These are implemented methods with separate evidence gates. The records below do not certify a single general end-to-end incident-to-production pipeline.

01 / INPUT

Incident IR

A configured extractor can propose structured claims from quoted text. Supplied reports can be fictional or owner-authored; saved responses can be replayed.

02 / SYSTEM

System IR

A frozen evidence-lineage adapter supplies System IR from captured code and specifications. Arbitrary repository-to-IR inference is not qualified. A Source cannot invent an unsupported contract.

03 / REVIEW

JEV assessment

JEV assesses whether claims match their quoted evidence. Support, contradiction and insufficient information remain separate. Its scores are not calibrated success probabilities.

04 / AUTHORITY

Binding & permission

Deterministic checks enforce supported callers, fixed copies, explicit approval, isolation and durable budgets. Missing support yields HELD or UNKNOWN.

05 / OBSERVATION

Fault → repair → recheck

Compare the original and candidate under fixed fault and normal conditions, then audit evidence. Adoption stays inside the authorized copy.
